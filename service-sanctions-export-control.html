Sanctions & Export Control

Forensic multi-tier beneficial ownership unwrapping, global sanctions watchlist audits, dual-use technology diversion tracing, and export license compliance across OFAC, EU, and UK regimes.

Rigorous Compliance for Global Capital & Trade Flows

In an era of weaponized trade controls, dynamic multilateral sanctions, and aggressive extraterritorial regulatory enforcement, superficial automated database screening is insufficient. Sanctioned sovereign entities and Specially Designated Nationals (SDNs) increasingly deploy intricate multi-jurisdictional shell corporations, nominee shareholders, and opaque trust vehicles to disguise commercial ownership.

Veritas Intelligence delivers forensic beneficial ownership deconstruction, supply chain diversion investigation, and regulatory exposure mapping. Our multi-disciplinary analysts blend deep open-source intelligence (OSINT), corporate registry filings across 140+ sovereign jurisdictions, maritime AIS telemetry, and confidential on-the-ground source inquiries to ensure cross-border mergers, supplier agreements, and trade financings withstand strict OFAC, EU, and UK scrutiny.

Sanctions Threats Countered

The 50% Rule & Aggregated Holdings Unraveling complex offshore SPVs, blind trusts, and intermediary holdings where sanctioned oligarchs or state-backed actors hold aggregated 50%+ equity without appearing on direct SDN lists.
Dual-Use Technology Diversion Tracking illicit transshipment hubs, front companies, and grey-market intermediaries in third-party jurisdictions routing restricted microelectronics or aerospace hardware into embargoed territories.
Secondary Sanctions & Financial Blockades Pre-empting catastrophic enforcement penalties, correspondent banking de-risking, and asset freezes triggered by transactions touching designated foreign financial institutions or blocked economic sectors.
Maritime Telemetry & Evasion Tactics Detecting AIS spoofing, dark-fleet STS (ship-to-ship) petroleum transfers, flag-hopping, and forged customs bills of lading designed to circumvent naval blockades and price cap restrictions.

Our 4-Stage Compliance & Sanctions Methodology

Stage 1

Mandate Scoping & Threat Calibration

Classifying transactions, export control classifications (ECCN / Dual-Use), and identifying exposure surfaces across OFAC, EU CFSP, UK Sanctions Act, and UN Security Council regimes.

Stage 2

Corporate Unwrapping & Registry Forensics

Deconstructing corporate ownership structures across offshore tax havens, identifying nominee directors, informal family proxies, and calculating aggregated beneficial ownership.

Stage 3

In-Country Source Inquiries & Trade Telemetry

Deploying lawful HUMINT networks, interviewing customs brokers and freight forwarders, and analyzing vessel AIS transponders to detect shadow diversion networks.

Stage 4

Privileged Defensibility Dossier & Remediation

Delivering audit-ready evidentiary reports tailored for general counsel, compliance committees, and regulatory enforcement authorities under strict attorney-client privilege.

Key Compliance Deliverables

  • Comprehensive 50% Rule Beneficial Ownership Dossier: Granular multi-jurisdictional ownership hierarchy trees detailing direct, indirect, and aggregate beneficial ownership percentages down to the ultimate natural person.
  • Dual-Use & Export Diversion Risk Matrix: Line-item classification of physical products against commerce control lists (EAR / ITAR / Military Goods) and risk scoring of intermediate transshipment hubs.
  • Privileged Legal Defense Briefing: Clear, evidentiary memorandum outlining identified red flags, license application viability, and tactical mitigation roadmaps for legal counsel.
