Practice Area Detail

Fraud & Forensic Financial Investigations

Methodical forensic accounting, balance sheet reconstruction, and transaction audits to uncover asset dissipation, embezzlement schemes, and falsified invoicing chains.

Uncovering Concealed Financial Malfeasance

Corporate fraud and complex financial misconduct can quietly drain millions in enterprise liquidity before standard audits detect the anomaly. Perpetrators frequently use collusive vendor networks, layered offshore shell entities, round-trip transactions, and sophisticated ledger manipulation to disguise systematic asset misappropriation.

Veritas Intelligence combines certified forensic accountants (CFEs), digital forensic technologists, and corporate investigators to trace missing funds and dismantle fraudulent schemes. We reconstruct corrupted general ledgers, recover deleted transaction records, and link siphoned capital directly to the beneficial owners responsible.

Critical Fraud Challenges Uncovered

Procurement Fraud & Fictitious Vendors Identifying shell companies issuing fraudulent invoices for non-existent goods or services with kickbacks to internal managers.
Executive Embezzlement & Asset Siphoning Tracing unauthorized wire transfers, inflated expense reimbursements, and diverted corporate investment opportunities.
Revenue Recognition & Financial Statement Fraud Uncovering premature booking of revenue, fictitious accounts receivable, and intentional concealment of commercial liabilities.
Cryptocurrency & Offshore Capital Flight Tracing diverted corporate assets across international banking corridors and decentralized cryptocurrency exchanges.

The Forensic Accounting Methodology

Phase 01 Day 1–3

Data Ingestion & Ledger Extraction

Forensic imaging of accounting software (SAP, Oracle, NetSuite), bank records, and accounts payable databases with cryptographic hashing.

Phase 02 Day 4–8

Algorithmic Pattern & Anomaly Detection

Applying Benford’s Law, duplicate transaction detection, out-of-sequence invoice matching, and round-amount payment audits to isolate red flags.

Phase 03 Day 9–14

Counterparty Tracing & Electronic Interviews

Subpoena-supported bank tracing, vendor address physical audits, and custodial witness interviews to establish intent and collusion.

Phase 04 Day 15

Certified Forensic Dossier & Restitution Strategy

Delivery of a trial-admissible expert forensic report complete with fund-flow diagrams and asset recovery recommendations.

Forensic Evidentiary Deliverables

  • Certified Forensic Accounting Report A rigorous, court-admissible financial audit report detailing reconstructed transaction ledgers, quantified damages, and identified fraud mechanisms.
  • Fund Flow Architecture Map Detailed visual schematics illustrating the step-by-step movement of misappropriated funds from corporate accounts into target-controlled entities.
  • Restitution & Recovery Playbook Actionable forensic evidence dossiers prepared to support emergency asset freezing orders, insurer claim submissions, or law enforcement referrals.

Chain of Custody & Expert Witness Standards
