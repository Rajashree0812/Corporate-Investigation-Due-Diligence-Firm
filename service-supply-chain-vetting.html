Practice Area Detail

Critical Supply Chain Vetting

Multi-tier supply chain integrity forensics, Tier-1 to Tier-3 vendor screening, Uyghur Forced Labor Prevention Act (UFLPA) origin tracing, and geopolitical bottleneck risk modeling.

Illuminating Blind Spots from Raw Extraction to Final Assembly

Modern enterprise supply chains are dangerously fragmented across multiple tiers of sub-contractors, brokers, and contract processing facilities. While primary Tier-1 suppliers maintain polished ESG credentials, critical compliance hazards—such as forced labor, illicit component substitution, and environmental liability—invariably lurk in opaque Tier-2 and Tier-3 layers.

Veritas Intelligence delivers forensic supply chain illumination. We combine forensic customs declarations, maritime bill of lading tracking, and unannounced on-the-ground factory inspections to establish verified custodial chain-of-custody. Our evidence packages withstand aggressive border customs scrutiny (including US CBP UFLPA detentions and EU Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive mandates) and insulate enterprises from disruptive supply halting.

Supply Chain Vulnerabilities Addressed

Sub-Tier (Tier-2 & Tier-3) Blind Spots Overcoming ERP limitations that fail to track raw material processors, foundries, and agricultural harvesters where serious integrity and compliance failures originate.
Forced Labor & UFLPA Port Detentions Preventing catastrophic border cargo seizures and customs withhold release orders (WROs) triggered by unverified supply origins or restricted manufacturing regions.
Counterfeiting & Gray-Market Substitution Uncovering illicit substitution of mil-spec, medical, or aerospace components with refurbished, cloned, or adulterated substandard materials.
Geopolitical Bottlenecks & Sole-Source Fragility Identifying hidden dependencies on single-point facilities located in conflict-vulnerable corridors, disputed maritime straits, or politically hostile nations.

Our 4-Stage Supply Chain Forensics Methodology

Stage 1

Multi-Tier Value Chain Decomposition

Dissecting product bills of materials (BOM), supplier transaction ledgers, and component dependencies across Tier-1, Tier-2, and Tier-3 manufacturing tiers.

Stage 2

Customs Manifest & Shipping Forensics

Cross-referencing maritime bills of lading, airway declarations, transshipment routes, and intermediary free trade zone storage logs to trace true origins.

Stage 3

Unannounced On-Site Inquiries & HUMINT

Deploying discreet local investigative teams to inspect processing plants, confirm workforce legitimacy, evaluate environmental controls, and interview suppliers.

Stage 4

Traceability Certification & Redundancy Blueprint

Synthesizing findings into court-admissible origin defense dossiers and vetted secondary supplier options in nearshore or allied trade jurisdictions.

Key Supply Chain Deliverables

  • Full-Chain Traceability & UFLPA Evidentiary Dossier: Defensible documentary audit trail verifying raw material origins, labor force integrity, and custodial chain of custody for regulatory clearance.
  • Tier-1 to Tier-3 Vendor Integrity Scorecard: Granular risk ratings assessing financial stability, sanctions exposure, corrupt patronage ties, and ESG compliance.
  • Supply Chain Chokepoint Resilience Blueprint: Concrete contingency roadmaps identifying verified alternative suppliers in allied jurisdictions to mitigate geopolitical disruptions.
