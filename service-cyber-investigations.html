Cyber Risk & Digital Forensics

Defensible host and cloud telemetry acquisition, trade secret exfiltration tracing, ransomware vector forensics, and court-admissible chain of custody reporting.

Defensible Evidence Acquisition for Critical Cyber Incidents

When enterprise networks are breached, proprietary algorithms copied, or departing executives suspected of data theft, general counsels and audit committees require fast, unimpeachable forensic intelligence. Digital forensic analysis must withstand rigorous cross-examination, judicial scrutiny, and adversarial technical audits in commercial courts.

Veritas Intelligence bridges technical cybersecurity data and legal dispute strategy. Our certified forensic examiners operate strict evidentiary protocols to capture volatile memory, extract cloud access telemetry, analyze compromised endpoints, and trace unauthorized data movements without altering underlying filesystem metadata or compromising attorney-client privilege.

High-Stakes Forensic Challenges Countered

Departing Executive Trade Secret Exfiltration Tracing bulk file downloads, personal USB storage connections, unauthorized cloud syncing (Dropbox, Google Drive), and email forwarders by key personnel.
Advanced Persistent Threat (APT) Intrusion Scoping Reconstructing initial access vectors, privilege escalation artifacts, lateral movements across Active Directory, and command-and-control beacons.
Ransomware & Extortion Vector Analysis Preserving encrypted volume artifacts, identifying exfiltrated database catalogs, and tracing cryptocurrency wallet transaction corridors for extortion attribution.
Anti-Forensics & Malicious Log Tampering Recovering purged event logs, detecting timestamp modification (timestomping), and extracting unallocated drive space artifacts to defeat evasion attempts.

The Cyber Forensic Investigation Lifecycle

Phase 01 Day 1–2

Live Triage & Bitstream Imaging

Non-invasive volatile RAM capture, hardware write-blocked physical bitstream disk imaging, and initial cryptographic dual-hash (MD5/SHA-256) logging.

Phase 02 Day 3–6

Multi-Cloud & Host Telemetry Correlation

Cross-referencing EDR logs, VPN authentication records, Microsoft 365 audit logs, and AWS CloudTrail trails to establish an uninterrupted chronological timeline.

Phase 03 Day 7–10

Artifact Analysis & Data Impact Scoping

Decompiling malicious scripts, inspecting Master File Table ($MFT) records, shellbags, and link files to determine precisely which confidential files were accessed or transferred.

Phase 04 Day 11+

Court-Admissible Dossier & Expert Witness Affidavits

Delivery of an exhaustive technical report, visual exfiltration schematics, and sworn expert witness affidavits tailored for judicial proceedings and regulatory notifications.

Tangible Forensic Deliverables

  • Cryptographic Hash Registry & Chain of Custody Record Complete verification logs validating the mathematical integrity of every physical and virtual image captured in accordance with ISO/IEC 27037 standards.
  • Breach Attribution & Exfiltration Timeline Report Visual second-by-second attack timeline, identifying initial ingress vectors, affected systems, compromised credentials, and definitive stolen file inventories.
  • Trial-Ready Expert Witness Testimony Dossier Sworn technical affidavits engineered to satisfy Federal Rule of Evidence 702 and civil procedural standards for preliminary injunctions and Anton Piller orders.

ISO/IEC 27037 & Attorney-Client Privilege

All investigative operations are executed under formal legal instruction to maintain attorney-client and work-product privilege. Our evidence handling protocols adhere strictly to ISO/IEC 27037 (Guidelines for identification, collection, acquisition and preservation of digital evidence) and NIST SP 800-86 standards, ensuring universal court admissibility across common law and civil law jurisdictions.
