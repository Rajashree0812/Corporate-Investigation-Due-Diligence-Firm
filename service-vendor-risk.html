Vendor Risk Management

Independent integrity audits, sanctions screening, and supply chain exposure analysis across Tier-1, Tier-2, and Tier-3 vendors, intermediaries, and global distributors.

Protecting Enterprise Value Across Global Supply Chains

In today’s hyper-connected and heavily regulated international commerce environment, multinational corporations are legally and financially accountable for the misconduct of their third-party suppliers, logistics partners, and local agents. Regulatory regimes such as the Uyghur Forced Labor Prevention Act (UFLPA), German Supply Chain Act (LkSG), and the EU CSDDD impose strict penalties for third-party infractions.

Veritas Intelligence provides end-to-end vendor risk auditing that penetrates beyond superficial self-assessment questionnaires. We deploy on-the-ground investigators and forensic analysts to verify physical facility operations, unmask hidden beneficial owners, and audit intermediaries against global sanctions, human rights abuses, and bribery networks.

Critical Third-Party Vulnerabilities Addressed

Concealed Sanctions & Transshipment Rerouting Detecting sanctioned components rerouted through intermediary jurisdictions to evade customs trade barriers and secondary sanctions.
Sub-Tier Supplier Obscurity (Tier-2 & Tier-3) Uncovering covert subcontracting to unapproved or blacklisted manufacturing hubs hidden behind prime vendors.
Foreign Agent Corruption & Bribery Exposure Auditing commercial intermediaries, customs clearing agents, and sales consultants for FCPA and UK Bribery Act exposure.
Labor Violations, Forced Labor & ESG Infractions Verifying workforce conditions, labor broker affiliations, and environmental compliance at physical overseas factories.

The Third-Party Diligence Protocol

Phase 01 Day 1–3

Automated Registry & Sanctions Screening

Screening supplier portfolios against 2,000+ global sanctions lists, adverse regulatory dockets, and state-owned enterprise (SOE) registers.

Phase 02 Day 4–8

UBO Unwrapping & Corporate Affiliation Mapping

Unraveling layered holding companies to verify whether foreign political figures or sanctioned parties maintain undisclosed equity in suppliers.

Phase 03 Day 9–12

On-the-Ground Site Audits & Verification

Physical facility inspections by local field investigators to confirm operational legitimacy, workforce authenticity, and production capacity.

Phase 04 Day 13–14

Risk Scoring & Mitigation Dossier

Delivery of an interactive risk scorecard classifying each vendor by compliance hazard level with clear remediative action steps.

Vendor Intelligence Deliverables

  • Vendor Integrity Scorecard & Matrix A comprehensive rating matrix grading suppliers on UBO transparency, regulatory compliance, sanctions risk, and labor integrity.
  • Sub-Tier Supply Chain Provenance Map Visual tracing of component origins, transshipment hubs, and logistics intermediaries to verify chain of custody.
  • Regulatory Compliance Certification Dossier Defensible audit documentation demonstrating reasonable due diligence under UFLPA, FCPA, and modern slavery disclosure statutes.

Statutory Alignment & Compliance Standards

Our methodology aligns directly with international supply chain compliance benchmarks including the OECD Guidelines for Multinational Enterprises, US Customs and Border Protection (CBP) WRO guidelines, and the UK Modern Slavery Act 2015, ensuring complete audit defensibility.
