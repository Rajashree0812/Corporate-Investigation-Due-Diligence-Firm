Who We Are

Illuminating Concealed Realities Across Global Markets

In an era of opaque offshore structures, cross-border jurisdictional friction, and sophisticated corporate malfeasance, conventional financial audits provide incomplete assurance.

Veritas Intelligence was formed to bridge the divide between corporate governance and actionable intelligence. We deploy specialized teams of former federal investigators, forensic accountants, intelligence analysts, and computer forensic specialists to unmask beneficial ownership, investigate allegations of corporate bribery, and support high-stakes commercial disputes.

Our Fiduciary Promise

We never speculate. Every finding presented in our intelligence dossiers is substantiated through verifiable primary source documentation and documented chain-of-custody protocols.

Guiding Principles

The Four Pillars of Veritas Intelligence

Our operational ethos guides every interview conducted, registry queried, and dossier compiled.

Integrity

Uncompromising ethical standards. We conduct all inquiries in strict compliance with the FCPA, UK Bribery Act 2010, GDPR, and relevant local statutory mandates.

Discretion

Absolute confidentiality. We operate under bilateral non-disclosure agreements with physical, logical, and cryptographic segregation of case files and findings.

Accuracy

Evidentiary precision. We triangulate findings across independent corporate registries, court records, and verified human sources before declaring analytical certainty.

Accountability

Defensibility in court. Our reports withstand rigorous judicial scrutiny, cross-examination by opposing counsel, and regulatory audit review.

Operational Framework

Admissible Evidentiary Standards & Legal Privilege

In high-stakes corporate disputes, how intelligence is gathered is as vital as the intelligence itself. We maintain an unbroken chain of custody, ensuring our findings can be introduced as admissible evidence in arbitrations and court trials.

Attorney-Client Privilege

Direct engagement by external trial counsel preserves work-product protection under applicable doctrine.

Clean Hands Doctrine

Zero tolerance for unlawful surveillance, hacking, or pretexting. Every report is ethically defensible.
