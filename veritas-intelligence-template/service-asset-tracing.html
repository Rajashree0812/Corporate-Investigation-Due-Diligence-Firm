Asset Tracing & Sovereign Asset Recovery

Global identification, mapping, and strategic freezing support for dissipated corporate capital, concealed real estate portfolios, offshore trust structures, and maritime assets.

Monetizing Judgments and Uncovering Concealed Wealth

Securing a multi-million-dollar arbitration award, commercial court judgment, or creditor claim is frequently only the first chapter of a protracted dispute. Sophisticated debtors routinely utilize labyrinthine offshore structures, nominee directors, blind trusts, and friendly sovereign jurisdictions to frustrate enforcement and conceal attachable assets.

Veritas Intelligence assists judgment creditors, international law firms, and sovereign states in transforming unenforceable paper judgments into tangible financial recoveries. We map undisclosed asset ownership across real estate, equity shares, maritime vessels, private aviation, and banking corridors to establish legal basis for worldwide freezing orders.

Sophisticated Debtor Strategies Countered

Layered Offshore Trusts & Nominee Directors Penetrating secretive corporate registries in jurisdictions such as BVI, Cayman, Panama, and Cyprus to expose true beneficial owners.
Concealed Real Estate & Luxury Physical Assets Locating high-value residential and commercial properties, corporate aircraft, and superyachts held through layered holding shells.
Pre-Judgment Fraudulent Conveyance & Asset Stripping Establishing documentary proof of emergency transfers made to family members or affiliated entities specifically to evade creditor claims.
Cross-Border Sovereign Immunity Barriers Identifying commercial, non-diplomatic assets of state-owned enterprises that are legally eligible for attachment under international law.

The Asset Tracing & Enforcement Lifecycle

Phase 01 Day 1–3

Judgment Scoping & Target Profiling

Analysis of judgment awards, underlying commercial disputes, known banking corridors, and debtor behavioral profiles across primary jurisdictions.

Phase 02 Day 4–9

Global Multi-Registry & Title Extraction

Exhaustive retrieval of corporate records, property deeds, maritime vessel registries (IMO tracking), aviation tail number logs, and patent databases.

Phase 03 Day 10–15

HUMINT & Banking Corridor Elicitation

Discreet source inquiries with industry counterparties, maritime brokers, and commercial lenders to uncover active transaction corridors.

Phase 04 Day 16

Evidentiary Asset Dossier & Freezing Order Affidavits

Delivery of a comprehensive asset recovery roadmap and sworn expert affidavits structured for immediate submission to commercial courts.

Tangible Recovery Deliverables

  • Global Asset Mapping Dossier & Schematics Visual relational charts linking the debtor to verified real estate, corporate equities, maritime vessels, and bank conduits globally.
  • Trial-Ready Affidavit in Support of Injunctions Meticulously referenced sworn evidence statements engineered to satisfy legal thresholds for Worldwide Freezing Orders (WFOs) and Mareva injunctions.
  • Multi-Jurisdiction Enforcement Strategy Memo Strategic guidance detailing which sovereign venues offer the fastest, most cost-effective legal mechanisms for asset seizure and liquidation.

Worldwide Freezing Orders & Admissibility

All intelligence is obtained through lawful, defensible investigative methodologies that comply with domestic and international privacy legislation. Our findings are structured to support Norwich Pharmacal disclosure orders, Bankers Trust orders, and Section 1782 US discovery applications without compromising cross-border legal privilege.
