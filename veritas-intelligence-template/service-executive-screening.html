Executive Screening & Leadership Due Diligence

Uncompromising background vetting on board nominees, prospective C-suite officers, and investment partners to protect enterprise reputation, capital, and governance.

Protecting Enterprise Value at the Leadership Level

Hiring or appointing a C-suite executive, board member, or joint venture principal without forensic-grade vetting is one of the greatest unhedged risks a corporation can incur. Conventional human resources background checks rely on self-reported disclosures and automated database checks that frequently fail to identify offshore corporate ties, sealed litigation, adverse foreign media, and covert regulatory investigations.

Veritas Intelligence delivers confidential, exhaustive leadership vetting to nomination committees, board chairs, and private equity sponsors. We delve into historical corporate directorships, beneficial ownership in undisclosed entities, civil and criminal records across 140+ countries, and discreet on-the-ground source inquiries to reveal an unvarnished assessment of executive integrity.

Critical Leadership Risks Mitigated

Undisclosed Entities & Corporate Conflicts Identifying secret ownership, conflicting directorships, or undeclared supplier ties that could trigger fiduciary or legal liability.
Regulatory Sanctions, Debarment & PEP Status Confirming whether candidates are politically exposed persons (PEPs) or have faced sanctions, export control, or securities enforcement actions.
Concealed Litigation & Adverse Civil Judgments Uncovering sealed or overseas litigation, contentious divorce asset disputes, and commercial fraud allegations that standard checks miss.
Resume Inflation & Embellished Track Record Verifying academic degrees, historical transaction milestones, executive equity exits, and employment timelines with primary sources.

The Executive Vetting Protocol

Phase 01 Day 1–2

Identity & Directorship Extraction

Verification of candidate identity, historical passports, and global corporate registry mapping to catalogue all corporate affiliations and directorships.

Phase 02 Day 3–5

Multi-Jurisdiction Docket & Regulatory Audit

Deep-dive retrieval of municipal, provincial, and national court registers, bankruptcy filings, tax liens, and regulatory enforcement histories across all lived territories.

Phase 03 Day 6–8

Reputational Profiling & Dark Web Intelligence

Forensic adverse media analysis across multi-language databases, social sentiment archives, corporate breach logs, and regulatory sanctions watchlists.

Phase 04 Day 9–10

Discreet Source Inquiries (HUMINT) & Dossier

Lawful, confidential interviews with former superiors, peers, and industry counterparts to synthesize verified character references into an executive dossier.

Comprehensive Vetting Deliverables

  • Executive Integrity Dossier A classified 30-to-60 page briefing compiling directorship trees, litigation docket registries, media sentiment indices, and factual integrity scorecards.
  • Global Regulatory & Sanctions Verification Official cross-checks against 2,000+ global sanctions lists, PEP databases, export enforcement boards, and judicial exclusion registers.
  • Human Source Intelligence Synthesis Discreet, anonymized peer and executive counterpart debriefs assessing governance track record, managerial ethics, and operational reputation.

Global Data Privacy & FCRA Compliance

Our executive vetting processes strictly adhere to the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA), General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and local jurisdictional privacy statutes. All intelligence is acquired through lawful, defensible open sources, verified registry filings, and voluntary human interviews without infringing on protected personal liberties.
