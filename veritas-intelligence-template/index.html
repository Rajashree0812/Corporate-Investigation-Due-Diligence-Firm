Defensible Methodology

The Four-Stage Investigative Lifecycle

Every inquiry adheres to verifiable standards of evidentiary integrity, strict legal privilege protocols, and systematic forensic rigor.

01

Consult

Initial confidential briefing under bespoke bilateral non-disclosure agreement. We define specific evidentiary parameters, legal privilege frameworks, and jurisdiction scope.

Bespoke Protocol & Scope Matrix
02

Assess

Preliminary threat assessment and data triangulation across corporate registries, regulatory filings, sanctioned entity lists, and court records across relevant jurisdictions.

Initial Red Flag Heatmap
03

Investigate

Multidimensional deep-dive combining open-source intelligence (OSINT), forensic balance-sheet forensics, digital footprints, and discreet on-the-ground source elicitation.

Primary Evidence & Chain of Custody
04

Report

Delivery of an exhaustive, court-admissible intelligence dossier alongside an executive briefing for board directors, legal counsel, and investment committees.
