Litigation Support & Evidence Research

Evidentiary support for international arbitration, commercial litigation, cross-border witness location, adversary asset intelligence, and trial-ready dossier preparation.

Independent Evidentiary Strategy for High-Stakes Commercial Disputes

In complex multi-jurisdictional litigation and international arbitration, legal arguments alone rarely dictate the outcome—the party that uncovers decisive, court-admissible facts controls settlement leverage and judicial determinations. Veritas Intelligence provides senior trial counsel, litigation boutiques, and corporate legal departments with rigorous, privilege-compliant investigative intelligence.

Operating strictly under attorney work-product protections and Upjohn standards, our investigative teams reconstruct complex factual timelines, uncover concealed evidence, profile adversary witnesses for impeachment, and unmask shadow litigation funding entities across offshore secrecy havens.

High-Stakes Dispute Challenges Countered

Hostile Witness Location & Credibility Profiling Tracing elusive material witnesses, uncovering prior contradictory testimony, undisclosed conflicts of interest, and regulatory enforcement histories for cross-examination.
International Commercial Arbitration Evidentiary Gaps Overcoming limited discovery rules in ICC, LCIA, SIAC, and ICSID proceedings by gathering extra-jurisdictional open-source intelligence and public record evidence.
Unmasking Third-Party Litigation Funding Identifying hidden litigation financiers, hedge fund backers, and shadow plaintiffs orchestrating corporate harassment or vexatious claims.
Adversary Solvency & Pre-Judgment Freezing Evidence Locating dissipated assets and verifying commercial adversary solvency to satisfy evidentiary standards for Mareva injunctions and worldwide freezing orders.

Our 4-Stage Evidentiary Methodology

Stage 1

Legal Scoping & Privilege Structuring

Aligning investigative mandates directly with trial counsel instructions under formal attorney work-product protections, ensuring all evidentiary trails remain confidential and legally shielded.

Stage 2

Cross-Border OSINT & Docket Reconstruction

Multi-jurisdictional retrieval of sealed filings, historical corporate filings, foreign court registers, maritime ship movements, and property registries across 140+ countries.

Stage 3

Discreet Human Intelligence & Field Inquiries

Deploying seasoned in-country forensic investigators to conduct discreet, lawful witness interviews, source intelligence, and fact-check contested adversarial assertions.

Stage 4

Court-Admissible Dossier & Expert Witness Reports

Synthesizing findings into rigorous, chronologically indexed hearing dossiers, sworn affidavits, and demonstrative visual exhibits that satisfy strict evidentiary admissibility rules.

Trial-Ready Deliverables

  • Comprehensive Witness Dossiers & Impeachment Binders: Detailed professional lineage, undisclosed business partnerships, financial liabilities, and verified public statements ready for deposition cross-examination.
  • Evidentiary Chain-of-Custody Documentation: Fully defensible digital and physical evidence handling logs conforming to international arbitral tribunal standards.
  • Demonstrative Tribunal Visuals & Timeline Charts: Clear, persuasive infographics and transaction relationship flowcharts clarifying convoluted fraud schemes for judges and arbitral panels.
