Mergers & Acquisitions Due Diligence

Pre-transaction investigative intelligence that identifies undisclosed beneficial owners, regulatory sanctions vulnerabilities, related-party conflicts, and hidden commercial liabilities before deal execution.

Executive Summary & Engagement Rationale

Standard accounting, tax, and legal due diligence typically evaluate data furnished willingly in the electronic data room by the target company. However, the most catastrophic deal risks frequently reside outside the data room: concealed nominee shareholders, offshore round-trip revenue schemes, unrecorded environmental liabilities, or hidden ties to sanctioned political figures.

Veritas Intelligence delivers independent, multi-jurisdictional pre-deal intelligence to investment committees, corporate acquirers, and cross-border buyout firms. We verify the true integrity of the target entity, its founding principals, and key commercial agreements across 140+ sovereign jurisdictions.

Critical Business Challenges Addressed

Concealed Beneficial Ownership (UBO) Uncovering politically exposed persons or sanctioned figures hiding behind nominee directors and offshore trusts.
Unrecorded Off-Balance Liabilities Identifying undisclosed commercial guarantees, pending regulatory investigations, and foreign civil disputes.
Customer Concentration & Related-Party Fraud Detecting artificial circular revenue generation between target executives and undisclosed corporate entities.
Secondary Sanctions & Bribery Exposures Evaluating historic corruption vulnerabilities that could trigger successor liability under the FCPA.

The Due Diligence Workflow

Phase 01 Day 1–3

Preliminary Scoping & Triage

Review of transaction targets, jurisdiction mapping, and electronic screening against global sanction, PEP, and regulatory enforcement databases.

Phase 02 Day 4–8

Deep Registry & Litigation Retrieval

On-the-ground acquisition of certified corporate filings, historical share allotments, court registers, and local civil litigation records in country.

Phase 03 Day 9–14

Human Source Inquiries (HUMINT)

Discreet, lawful interviews with industry peers, former executives, customers, and suppliers to verify reputation, market standing, and operational realities.

Phase 04 Day 15

Evidentiary Dossier & Board Briefing

Delivery of a classified Red Flag Matrix, comprehensive evidentiary source appendix, and direct partner debriefing for deal counsel and M&A heads.

Concrete Engagement Deliverables

  • Comprehensive Due Diligence Dossier A fully footnoted 40-to-120 page intelligence report indexing all corporate shareholding trees, litigation indices, and verified background findings.
  • Red Flag Executive Summary & Risk Heatmap A high-impact executive visual matrix categorizing identified risks by severity, operational impact, and probability to inform purchase price adjustments.
  • Certified Documentary Evidence Binder Original primary filings, certified court records, property deeds, and corporate registry filings delivered cryptographically.

Privilege Protection & Evidentiary Integrity

When Veritas Intelligence is retained directly by your external legal counsel to support legal advice in anticipation of an M&A transaction or dispute, our reports and work product can be structured to fall under attorney-client privilege and work-product doctrine in recognized jurisdictions.
